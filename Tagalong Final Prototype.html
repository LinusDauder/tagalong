Tagalong prototype

Rooms are locked until you scan the QR (or open the link) that comes with a ticket. It opens a welcome screen, you register and you land inside that event. Each festival or concert is its own room with its own people and look. Open the menu at the top left to switch between them. Tap the left or right side of a photo to see more of that person. Arrow keys swipe.

Ticket QRs

Scan one with your phone, or click it here to simulate the scan. Each QR opens the welcome screen of that event.

Profile photos

People have 3 to 10 generated photos each and groups up to 15 (a group shot plus at least two per member), in different scenes (sport, walks, travel, pets, nights out). Pick photos from your device and they are spread across the profiles. The more you add, the fewer repeats. Photos stay in your browser.
